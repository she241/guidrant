Safety by design

Reduce fraud before a seeker pays or visits.

Guidrent should not treat a marketplace badge as a guarantee. A production launch needs its own verification, reporting and moderation process.

Agent verification
ID, selfie, business information and phone ownership checks.
Listing review
Duplicate image checks, rent-term disclosure and source evidence.
Tour protection
Shareable itinerary, check-in and trusted-contact options.
Payment warning
Never send rent before identity, authority and property access are verified.
