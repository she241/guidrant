Privacy Notice

How Guidrent collects, uses, stores and protects personal information.

Draft effective date: 27 July 2026 · Owner details to complete before launch: legal entity name, registered address, support email and privacy contact.

1. Who controls your information

Guidrent is operated by [INSERT LEGAL ENTITY NAME] of [INSERT REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Questions and privacy requests should be sent to [INSERT PRIVACY EMAIL]. This document is a launch-ready draft and must be reviewed against the final business structure, vendors and data flows.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email, telephone number, profile photograph and role.
  • Seeker preferences such as budget, preferred areas, move-in date and saved properties.
  • Agent information such as agency, public contact details, service areas and verification documents.
  • Property listing information, photographs, location, amenities, pricing and availability.
  • Messages, tour requests, applications, support communications, reports and reviews.
  • Security and technical information such as device/browser details, sign-in records and consent choices.

3. Why we use information

We use information to provide the marketplace, authenticate users, match seekers with properties, enable tours and messaging, review agents and listings, prevent fraud, respond to support and privacy requests, improve reliability and comply with lawful obligations.

4. Sensitive documents

Identity, verification and rental-application documents are stored in private storage and should be made available only to authorised users. Do not upload documents unless the receiving agent or property manager has explained why they are needed. Guidrent should establish a documented retention period before launch.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with the agent or seeker involved in a transaction, service providers that host or deliver the platform, professional advisers, regulators or law-enforcement bodies where lawfully required. Guidrent does not sell personal information.

6. International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside Ghana. Before launch, Guidrent must document those providers, their locations, contractual safeguards and retention practices.

7. Retention

Account and marketplace records are retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described, fraud prevention, dispute handling and lawful recordkeeping. A final retention schedule must be approved before production launch.

8. Your choices and rights

Users can update profiles, control optional alerts, download data through the dashboard when the API is enabled, or submit access, correction, objection, export and deletion requests. Some records may need to be retained where deletion would interfere with fraud prevention, disputes or legal duties.

9. Security

Guidrent uses role-based access, row-level database rules, private document storage, encryption in transit, audit logging and account verification. No online service is completely risk-free, so users should protect passwords and report suspicious activity promptly.

10. Children

Guidrent is intended for adults who can enter housing arrangements. The service should not knowingly collect accounts from children.

11. Ghana privacy framework

The production service should be registered and operated in line with applicable Ghana privacy requirements, including the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and guidance from the Data Protection Commission. This page is not a substitute for legal advice or registration.

12. Changes

Material changes will be dated and communicated through the service. The policy version accepted by a signed-in user can be recorded in the database.
