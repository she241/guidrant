Safety Centre

Practical steps for avoiding housing scams and unsafe tours.

Draft effective date: 27 July 2026 · Owner details to complete before launch: legal entity name, registered address, support email and privacy contact.

Before contacting an agent

  • Compare the price with similar homes and be cautious of pressure or unusually low offers.
  • Review the agent profile, verification status and source information.
  • Do not share identity or financial documents before understanding who will receive them and why.

Before a tour

  • Confirm the exact address, agent name and appointment time.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going and share the listing.
  • Meet during daylight where possible and avoid isolated locations alone.
  • Use a live video tour first when distance or safety is a concern.

Before paying

  • Inspect the property and confirm the person’s authority to let it.
  • Review a written tenancy agreement and all rent, advance, deposit and fee terms.
  • Use traceable payment methods and obtain a written receipt.
  • Do not pay merely to “hold” a property because another renter is allegedly waiting.

Report concerns

Use the listing report function or contact [INSERT SAFETY EMAIL]. Preserve screenshots, payment records and messages. For immediate danger contact the appropriate local emergency authority.

What verification means

Verification indicates that specified information was reviewed at a point in time. It does not guarantee property ownership, future conduct, availability or the outcome of a transaction.
